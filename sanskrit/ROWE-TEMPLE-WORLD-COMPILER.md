That is exactly what we have been trying to do with Sanskrit, Bruno and StoneDoorway.

Rowe explicitly describes his Enochian Temple method as transforming the **2-D Elemental Tablet into a 3-D structure**. The Tablet's hierarchy determines which squares become floor, pillars, ceiling, roof, central axis, rings, altars, etc.

And he expects the practitioner to become able to maintain the Temple mentally and **move around inside and outside it without difficulty**.

That is the bit we should steal wholesale.

## Rowe's real innovation

Abstractly, his procedure is:

**symbolic matrix**  
→ identify hierarchy  
→ give every class of symbol a geometric role  
→ extrude 2D relations into 3D  
→ inhabit it  
→ activate sections independently  
→ manipulate the architecture  
→ observe resulting experiential changes.

For example, the basic Enochian square becomes a **cube**. Groups of squares retain their source relationships when reorganized as pillars, floors, crosses and other structures. Relative dimensions are fixed by the source system rather than chosen decoratively.

That's very different from a normal memory palace.

A normal palace says:

> “Put fact X on this table because you'll remember the table.”

Rowe says, effectively:

> **“The building itself is the information.”**

That's enormously relevant to what we're building.

# Rowe gives us another brilliant idea: activation state

In Rowe's consecration description the Temple is initially visualized almost like a **skeleton**. As particular forces are invoked, their corresponding architectural parts light up or become energized.

This is perfect for learning.

Your Sanskrit world shouldn't appear fully populated on day one.

Initially:

**grey / dormant architecture.**

You actually learn `ka`.

Now its cell activates.

You distinguish:

`k / kh / g / gh / ṅ`

The entire velar row lights up.

You learn `ac`.

A path/ring connecting the relevant sound classes becomes active.

You discover √gam.

Its object/agent appears.

You correctly derive or recognize a form five times.

That object's relationships become more vivid.

You encounter it in an actual verse.

Now a **route between the grammar structure and the verse-room** appears.

So the world visually represents **what you have actually internalized**.

That's much more meaningful than XP bars.

# And Rowe's mutable Temple is even more useful

In *The Lotus of the Temple*, Rowe changes the **placement of the altars**, and this changes the shape and behavior of the larger imagined field surrounding the Temple.

Forget the occult-energy claim for a moment.

As interface design, this is excellent:

> **moving one symbolic component changes the state of the entire environment.**

That is precisely what a grammatical operator should do.

Suppose √gam is standing in your dhātu chamber.

Apply:

**ā-**

and the space/motion orientation changes.

Apply:

**ṇic**

and now another agent appears who causes the motion.

Apply:

**kta**

and the dynamic movement collapses into a resultant/completed state.

The *world* changes rather than a tooltip saying:

> “ṇic = causative suffix.”

That's Rowe's logic applied to Pāṇini.

# A Sanskrit temple almost designs itself

Not a fake “ancient Sanskrit Temple of Bruno.”

A deliberately modern mnemonic architecture whose geometry is derived from Sanskrit.

## Level 1 — Mātṛkā / sound floor

Start from the thing we're currently learning:

the varṇa grid.

Instead of an arbitrary room:

```text
                MANNER →

PLACE ↓     unvoiced   aspirated   voiced   voiced-asp   nasal

velar           k          kh         g          gh        ṅ
palatal         c          ch         j          jh        ñ
retroflex       ṭ          ṭh         ḍ          ḍh        ṇ
dental          t          th         d          dh        n
labial          p          ph         b          bh        m
```

Now **extrude it** Rowe-style.

Every cell becomes a small chamber/cube.

Personal:

colour  
resonance  
body sensation  
movement  
rhythm

gets installed there.

Walking sideways changes **manner**.

Walking vertically changes **place of articulation**.

So you can literally navigate:

`ta → tha → da → dha → na`

and feel only one dimension changing.

That's a superb internal representation.

## Above that: The Māheśvara ring

Instead of dumping the 14 Māheśvara Sūtras onto the same floor, give them another geometry.

Put them around the sound temple as a **circumferential ring / arcade**:

```text
a i u ṇ
ṛ ḷ k
e o ṅ
ai au c
...
```

Now a **pratyāhāra becomes a traversable arc**.

When you invoke/think:

**ac**

you don't remember a list.

The relevant span of the architecture illuminates.

That is essentially converting Pāṇini's compressed addressing language into **spatial addressing**.

You could close your eyes and ask:

> `ik`

and experience the appropriate region activating.

That is exactly the sort of thing Rowe's system is good at.

That is very Brunian.

## Verse rooms should be temporary structures

This is perhaps the best use of Rowe.

Every verse doesn't need permanent palace space.

You enter a **compiler chamber**.

Take:

**asato mā sad gamaya**

The raw sound enters first.

Then the environment decomposes it.

You watch:

`asataḥ + mā`

produce:

`asato mā`

Elsewhere:

`sad + gamaya`

becomes:

`sad gamaya`.

√gam is already a known inhabitant from previous work.

Now the causative operator activates.

The verse ultimately resolves spatially into:

```text
ASAT  ─────────→  SAT

TAMAS ─────────→  JYOTIS

MṚTYU ─────────→  AMṚTA

          ↑
      GAMAYA
    same movement
```

You've now **inhabited the grammar of the verse**.

Then you leave.

But the discoveries are compiled back into your permanent world.

That's exactly the original idea:

> every Sanskrit verse teaches us something new about the machine.

## One distinction to preserve

Keep **actual Enochian Temple practice** separate from the Sanskrit architecture.

Rowe himself presents his Temple as an occult instrument tied specifically to Enochian Tablets and their names/forces. He even warns that he considers the practice powerful and unsuitable for beginners.

So:

**Rowe-derived world compiler**  
≠  
**performing an Enochian invocation.**

We can use the former freely as cognitive architecture.

Then if we separately want an **actual Enochian world package**, we can preserve Rowe's system almost literally:

```text
Enochian Tablet
↓
Rowe transformation rules
↓
3-D Temple
↓
StoneDoorway world
```

No Bruno or Sanskrit reinterpretation required.

That would actually be incredible in StoneDoorway because Rowe already gives us nearly everything necessary: dimensions, relationships, pillars, floor, altars, rings, light behavior, traversal and activation sequences.

And because Hermetic Library has images of Rowe-style Temple reconstructions, we'd have visual validation while building it.
