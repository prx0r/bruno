## One-line method

```text
sound → verse → discovery → operator → prediction → new verse → revised model → Trika
```

**Not:** alphabet → grammar → vocabulary → literature.

**Step 1 is phonemes/varṇas** — but **do not** spend a month on drills before real Sanskrit.
**~7–10 days sound-body**, then short texts immediately; refine pronunciation *through* texts.

## Why sound first

Sanskrit sound system is organized by **place and manner of articulation**. Later grammatical changes depend on those classes.
Reference: [Learn Sanskrit — points of pronunciation](https://www.learnsanskrit.org/guide/sounds/points-of-pronunciation/) (verified live)

Points: **kaṇṭha** (throat/velar) · **tālu** (palate) · **mūrdhan** (retroflex) · **danta** (teeth) · **oṣṭha** (lips) · nāsikā · jihvāmūlam.

Within each stop-series:

**unvoiced/unaspirated → unvoiced/aspirated → voiced/unaspirated → voiced/aspirated → nasal**

**ka kha ga gha ṅa** = one body place + five transformations — not five arbitrary syllables.

### Colour / phenomenology rule

| Dimension | Structure |
|-----------|-----------|
| Place family | colour-field A–E (velar/palatal/retroflex/dental/labial) |
| Aspiration | expansion / breath burst |
| Voicing | vibration |
| Nasality | resonance upward/inward |

**Do not** assign 50 unrelated colours. **Articulatory topology first**, then your subjective layer.

**Week-1 goal:** hear **ta / ṭa** and *feel* the difference without verbal translation.

## Stage details

### Days 1–7 — sound-body

Do **not** memorize alphabetically. Learn Sanskrit’s geometry (places above).
End of week: **ta vs ṭa** felt without translation.

### Days 5–14 — Māheśvara Sūtras as music

Chant all fourteen (TSYP has recordings):

```text
a i u ṇ · ṛ ḷ k · e o ṅ · ai au c · ...
```

These are **compressed sound indexing**, not only mysticism.
Ranges → **pratyāhāras** (`ac` = vowels class, `hal` = consonants class).

**When you hear `ac`, the internal vowel field should light up.**

### Verse 1 — Asato mā sad gamaya (week 2)

```text
asato mā sad gamaya
tamaso mā jyotir gamaya
mṛtyor mā amṛtaṃ gamaya
```

**Pass 1 phenomenology only:** sound · mouth · breath · rhythm · colour · movement. **No English.**

Then decompile:
- **√gam** = motion  
- **gamaya** ≈ first major morphological operator (causative “lead / cause to go”)  
- Architecture **SOURCE → DEST → MOTION** ×3 → Brunian machine with three inputs  

Then AI variants → **predict** = first RL loop.

### Verse 2 — Sarve bhavantu sukhinaḥ (week 3)

```text
sarve bhavantu sukhinaḥ
sarve santu nirāmayāḥ...
```

**√bhū** becoming/being · √as · person/number/mood · easy rhythm.

Now two agents: **√gam motion** · **√bhū becoming**.

### Verse 3 — Vakratuṇḍa mahākāya (week 4)

Compounds as **fused entities** (Bruno operation):

- vakra + tuṇḍa  
- mahā + kāya  
- sūrya + koṭi + sama + prabha  

**√kṛ** via *kuru*.

Three mega-roots: **√gam · √bhū · √kṛ**.

### Then leave chanting-only Sanskrit

After three chants → **ordinary Classical** via simple *subhāṣitas* (avoid “devotional formula decoder” trap).

Example:

```text
vidyā dadāti vinayaṃ
vinayād yāti pātratām...
```

**√dā** · **√yā** · case chain as argument structure:

**vidyā → vinaya → pātratā → dhana → dharma → sukha**

### Then Trika directly

**Śiva Sūtras** — gloriously tiny:

| Sūtra | Approx | Installed |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **1.1 caitanyam ātmā** | Consciousness is the Self | caitanya · ātman · nominal relation |
| **1.2 jñānaṃ bandhaḥ** | Knowledge is bondage (text’s technical sense) | √jñā |

Later **Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam 1**:

**citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ**

Decompile: sounds → sandhi → stems → morphology → compound → syntax → conceptual architecture. Philosophy encoded because you care.

## Daily pattern (~60 min)

| Time | Block |
|------|-------|
| **10 min** | Phoneme drills — one articulation family, slow, felt |
| **10 min** | Māheśvara Sūtras — chant + random query (`ac`? `hal`?) |
| **15 min** | Today’s verse — **sound only** / shadow |
| **20 min** | Decompile with AI — roots, endings, sandhi, operators |
| **10 min** | Brunian reconstruction — close everything; rebuild verse |
| **5 min** | Generation game — change one root/operator/case; predict |

**Start Asato Mā on day 3–4.** Don’t wait for “perfect” phonemes.
