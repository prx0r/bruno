## What Goswami actually teaches (practice grammar)

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Laya** | Absorption — reabsorption of emitted into source (not destruction) |
| **1:4:2** | puraka : kumbhaka : rechaka — classical 16 : 64 : 32 **units**, reduce if needed |
| **Sahita** | Breath-control *with* inhalation/exhalation — **this is tonight’s path** |
| **Kewala** | Natural breathless suspension — later outcome, not forced tonight |
| **Suṣumṇā** | Central channel; forms when ida/pingala absorb in kumbhaka (advanced description) |
| **Bandhas** | mūla · jalandhara · uddiyana — used with sahita kumbhaka for rousing; **optional at night** |
| **Stages** | perspiration → shaking → lightness — appear after long regular practice, not goals to chase |
| **Mantra** | “Aspect of kuṇḍalinī in sound-form” — chant can ride breath later |

**Reduction rule (Goswami / tantric sources):** if you cannot do 16-64-32, use **4-16-8**; if still hard, **1-4:2**.

**Tonight’s default rung:** **4-16-8** (or **2-8-4** if new) · 5 rounds alternate · then both-nostril soft 1:4:2 ×3.

## Session A — Core nightly (25–35 min) · DO THIS TONIGHT

### 0. Seat (2 min)
- Spine long, shoulders soft, jaw unclenched  
- No heavy meal · not while driving  
- Optional: Stonedoorway quiet bed (finish OFF)  

### 1. Natural elongation (3 min) — *laya prep, not force*
1. Inhale through both nostrils until lungs ~70% full (not max)  
2. **Elongate the exhale** — soft, unforced, longer than inhale  
3. 8–10 breaths  
4. Notice: can the exhale extend without strain? **That is the elongation skill.**  

*Goswami frame:* rechaka is where control develops. Don’t chase retention yet.

### 2. Nāḍī śodhana · 1:4:2 (8–12 min) — *suṣumṇā prep*
**Alternate nostril, ratio 1:4:2.**

One round:
```text
close right → inhale LEFT   = 1 unit
close left  → hold          = 4 units
close left  → exhale RIGHT  = 2 units
close right → inhale RIGHT  = 1 unit
close left  → hold          = 4 units
close right → exhale LEFT   = 2 units
```

| Rung | Inhale | Hold | Exhale | Use when |
|------|--------|------|--------|----------|
| **2-8-4** | 2s | 8s | 4s | First weeks / tight chest |
| **4-16-8** | 4s | 16s | 8s | **Default tonight** |
| 16-64-32 | 16s | 64s | 32s | Only after weeks stable (classical) |

**Do:**
- **5 rounds** alternate at your rung  
- Bandhas: **none tonight** unless you already practice them safely  
- Soft jalandhara (chin slightly down) only if it doesn’t compress breath  
- After 5 rounds: both nostrils open, soft **both-nostril 1:4:2 ×3** (same ratio)  
- **45–60s silence** — stop counting; notice equal flow / warmth / settle  

*What you’re training:* balance ida/pingala → conditions for suṣumṇā; elongation inside the ratio (exhale always 2× inhale).

### 3. Breath as mantra — VBT breath layer (5–7 min) — *optional tonight*
Pick **one** āṇavopāya breath dhāraṇā (from VB mapping):

| # | Technique | Do |
|---|-----------|-----|
| **4** | **Watch the turn** | Inhale/exhale; place attention on the **pause between** out and in (and heart void). Don’t manufacture the pause. |
| **1** | **Stop breath — feel unfilled void** | After a soft exhale, rest in the empty space *without straining hold*. If uncomfortable, skip. |
| **14** | **Heart → 12 fingers above** | On breath, feel attention travel heart → above crown; return. Light visualization. |

**Rules:**  
- No forced retention · no panic · no “must feel kundalini”  
- Lakshmanjoo note in mapping: some VB techniques are for masters — **use breath-based ones gently**  
- If one technique feels wrong, switch or stop  

### 4. Laya absorption (5 min) — *the point of the night*
1. Stop all manipulation  
2. Sit or lie · natural breath  
3. Attention on **one** of:  
   - breath at nostrils  
   - heart (green core)  
   - a single mātṛkā sound if you chanted earlier  
4. When mind wanders, return without drama  
5. Goal is **absorption into stillness**, not visions  

*Goswami laya:* multiform attention → bindu (point) → source. Tonight = practice the **return**, not the miracle.

### 5. Close (2 min)
- Hands on belly · 3 natural breaths  
- Stand slowly  
- Optional 1-line log (ochema diary or notebook):  
  `ratio | rounds | elongation quality | what settled | any tension locus`

## Progression path (weeks → advanced tantric work)

| Phase | Focus | Rung / practice | Done when |
|-------|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Week 1–2** | Elongation + balance | 2-8-4 or 4-16-8 · 5 rounds · no bandhas | Exhale elongates without strain |
| **Week 3–4** | Stable 4-16-8 | Same + both-nostril ×3 + laya silence | 5 rounds calm · nostrils feel even |
| **Month 2** | Add light jalandhara | Soft chin lock on hold only | Hold comfortable · no jaw/throat grip |
| **Month 3** | Optional mūla bandha | Gentle, only during kumbhaka | No pelvic strain |
| **Later** | Classical 16-64-32 · bhastrika · uddiyana | **Only with clear stability / teacher** | Perspiration/lightness appear as *byproducts*, not targets |
| **Then** | Mantra-on-breath · kuṇḍalinī concentration · VB breath set | After sahita is automatic | Kewala becomes natural, not forced |

**Goswami on stages:** perspiration → shaking → levitation appear after **long regular practice**. Tonight you train the **condition**, not the stage.

| Avoid | Why |
