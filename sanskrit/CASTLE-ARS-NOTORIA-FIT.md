## What Castle fixes / reweights

### Drop as core (again, with scholarship)
- Solomonic **Goetic circles, robes, fumigations** as StoneDoorway identity  
- Treating Notoria as “just another grimoire full of spirit commands”

### Elevate
- **Notoria = faculty engine** (memory · intellect · eloquence · understanding)  
- **Notae = discipline figures** → one room/art  
- **Method of places = Bruno-compatible** — imprinting/storing/retrieval is the *same cognitive stack*  
- **Abbreviated practice** as honest accessibility — matches “extract what you need”  
- **Angel-guide** pedagogy → StoneDoorway voice Guide (labelled, non-dogmatic)

### Keep honest
- Christian liturgical content is **historically true** in Castle — we don’t strip it silently; we **optionalize** it  
- Voces magicae / angelic names ≠ Sanskrit bīja — **related by “sacred sound form,” not by identity**  
- Figure geometry ≠ our Caelum math — complementary, not identical  
- Version A/B/C chaos → we privilege **method structure**, not woodcut reproduction

## What this means for StoneDoorway

Castle’s **operation** (not the full medieval regime):

```text
INTENTION     one faculty / one art (memory | eloquence | understanding)
PURIFICATION  optional: quiet body, clear intention (not costume)
FIGURE        open discipline hub (nota / room)
INSPECTION    contractio — hold image + sound + locus
PRAYER/VOICE  optional liturgical text OR your own Sanskrit chant
GUIDE         AI in ears: “you are now entering the grammar room…”
WORK          chant varṇa row · walk Caelum · derive one form
RETURN        log what stuck — memory/intellect/eloquence
```

**Reframe (keep):**  
StoneDoorway is **not** primarily summoning external entities.  
Castle supports **angel-as-teacher / faculty-development** as the historical core of Notoria.
